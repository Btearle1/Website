System

Reverse-engineering the system

The system arrived with minimal documentation, so I rebuilt its architecture from the hardware and source. I mapped how the Python/Flask web interface, the ESP32 serial pan/tilt link and the GPIO stepper control fit together.

Housing

Redesigning the laser housing

The original laser module housing had design flaws that affected how the laser mounted and aligned. I redesigned it in Autodesk Fusion and 3D printed the new version.

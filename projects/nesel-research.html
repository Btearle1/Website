My role

What I do

  • System analysis and documentation. I took over a lab system that had almost no documentation, then traced and documented how its hardware and software work together, so the lab could maintain and extend it.
  • Hardware redesign. I found flaws in a mechanical component that affected the system's accuracy, then redesigned it in CAD and 3D printed the replacement.
  • Software development. I'm building new features for the system's web-based control interface.
